3. Hard safeguards identify conditions in which an executable action should not be recommended automatically.
4. Soft safeguards adjust the relative preference of an action without making it infeasible.
5. Missing or conflicting evidence is not treated as evidence that an action is safe.
6. Investigate is treated as a manual-review outcome rather than an automatically executed marketing action.
