NYKAA CAMPAIGN DATA — THREE-LAYER DESIGN

1. RAW DATASET
- File: 01_raw_dataset_55555x16.csv
- 55,555 rows and original 16 columns.
- Exact source copy. No reviewer labels or calculated columns are added.

2. PROCESSED MODEL DATA
- File: 02_processed_model_data.csv
- Retains all original fields and adds non-destructive calculated fields.
- CTR = Clicks / Impressions.
- Click_to_Lead_Rate = Leads / Clicks.
- Lead_to_Conversion_Rate = Conversions / Leads.
- Overall_Conversion_Rate = Conversions / Impressions.
- Estimated_Total_Acquisition_Cost = Acquisition_Cost × Conversions. This is explicitly a proxy because the source does not define whether Acquisition_Cost is total or per acquisition.
- ROAS_Proxy = Revenue / Estimated_Total_Acquisition_Cost.
- Calculated_ROI_Proxy = (Revenue - Estimated_Total_Acquisition_Cost) / Estimated_Total_Acquisition_Cost.
- Performance_Score is a weighted quartile-normalized score: 30% source ROI, 25% overall conversion, 20% CTR, 15% lead-to-conversion, 10% engagement.
- Raw source fields are never overwritten.

3. GOLD BENCHMARK
- File: 03_gold_benchmark_125.csv
- 125 rows sampled across 5 campaign types and 5 action outcomes: Scale, Optimize, Monitor, Test, Pause.
- Contains original campaign attributes plus two reviewer label sets, adjudicated labels, and status.

INTENT RULES
- Email normally indicates Retention/Re-engagement; exceptionally strong conversion can make Sales Conversion the dominant intent.
- Paid Ads normally indicate Traffic Generation or Sales Conversion depending on downstream conversion strength.
- SEO normally indicates Brand Awareness or Traffic Generation; very strong post-click progression can indicate Lead Generation.
- Social Media and Influencer normally indicate Brand Awareness or Traffic Generation; strong conversion plus ROI can indicate Sales Conversion.

ACTION RULES
- Scale Budget: ROI and overall conversion are top-quartile, with at least median engagement.
- Pause and Review: ROI and overall conversion are both bottom-quartile.
- Refresh Creative/Targeting: CTR is bottom-quartile but downstream lead quality is acceptable.
- Optimize Landing Page: CTR is acceptable but click-to-lead is bottom-quartile.
- Improve Offer/Checkout: lead generation is acceptable but lead-to-conversion is bottom-quartile.
- Maintain and Monitor: ROI and conversion are above median but below scaling criteria.
- Run Controlled Test: mixed results without a single dominant bottleneck.

REVIEW PROCESS
- Reviewers use the same evidence but can differ on adjacent, defensible interpretations.
- Disagreements are deterministic and limited; no random impossible labels are inserted.
- Annotation_Status is Agreed only when both reviewers match the final adjudicated intent and action; otherwise it is Adjudicated.
